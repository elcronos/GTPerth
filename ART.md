# ART — "Perth golden hour, Leonida grade"
Thesis: T4 desert-dusk adapted to clear coastal air. Sun WSW (bearing ≈245°), 10.5° up, setting over the Narrows /
Kings Park as seen from Elizabeth Quay. Warm orange key, teal-blue shadows, pink-violet zenith, low haze so the
2 km skyline from South Perth still reads. Signature surface: Perth Water's sun glitter path; tinted curtain walls
reflecting the sunset with per-panel distortion and interior-mapped offices.
Palette: sun #FFB15C, glass teal-grey, EQ granite/limestone pavers, copper (Bell Tower), white steel (bridge, Spanda).
Hero shots: (1) bridge into the sun toward the Narrows, (2) spawn at The Landing, (3) MV Tricia at EQ Jetty,
(4) skyline from Mends St, (5) ferry chase on Perth Water, (6) Bell Tower + Ritz in full sun.
